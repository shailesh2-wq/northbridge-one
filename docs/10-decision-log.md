# Decision Log — NorthBridge One

> [!NOTE]
> Based on secondary research and simulated stakeholder discovery. No real interviews were conducted. This is a portfolio prototype.

| # | Problem | Evidence / Context | Decision | Feature / Architecture | Expected Outcome | Metric to Monitor |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Teachers miss warning signs because data is siloed. | Stakeholders report checking 3-4 systems to understand a student's situation. | **Centralize student data into a single view.** | Student 360 Profile | Teachers can access full context instantly without switching apps. | Time to access student context; App switching frequency. |
| **2** | Important issues fall through the cracks even when identified. | Interventions are currently tracked in emails or personal notebooks, lacking accountability. | **Implement a structured task management system.** | Action Center & Intervention Logging | Interventions are tracked, assigned, and resolved transparently. | Intervention Closure Rate. |
| **3** | Users suffer from information overload when viewing raw data. | Dashboards with too many charts overwhelm users and hide critical anomalies. | **Adopt an exception-based management UI.** | Attention Signals Engine | Users only see what requires immediate action, reducing cognitive load. | Click-through rate on Attention Signals. |
| **4** | Parents feel disconnected and overwhelmed by multiple communication channels. | Parents report missing emails and struggling to navigate complex portals. | **Provide a unified activity feed for parents.** | Parent App Activity Feed | Parents have a single, chronological timeline of their child's school life. | Parent Engagement Score (Weekly active users). |
| **5** | Teachers find data entry tedious and time-consuming. | Attendance takes away from teaching time; compliance is difficult to enforce. | **Optimize high-frequency tasks for speed.** | 1-Click Attendance Module | Teachers spend less time on admin, compliance increases. | Average time to submit attendance; Missing registers. |
| **6** | Leadership lacks real-time operational visibility. | Principals rely on weekly or termly reports to understand school health. | **Aggregate data upward in real-time.** | Role-Based Leadership Dashboards | Principals can identify and address systemic issues as they happen. | Frequency of leadership dashboard logins. |
| **7** | We cannot replace all legacy systems at once. | The school has long-term contracts with existing SIS and accounting software. | **Act as an integration layer first, replacement second.** | Core Integrations (One-way sync) | Faster time-to-market and lower initial friction for adoption. | Successful daily data sync rate. |
| **8** | Staff need to know the 'why' behind an alert. | Black-box algorithms are often mistrusted by educators. | **Ensure all signals are explainable.** | Transparent Signal Logic | High trust and utilization of the alerting system. | User feedback on signal relevance. |
| **9** | Communication between school and home is fragmented. | Emails, SMS, and paper notes create a disjointed record. | **Centralize messaging within the platform context.** | Unified Messaging System | Complete history of parent-school communication is preserved. | Volume of messages sent via platform vs external email. |
| **10** | Building native mobile apps is too resource-intensive for MVP. | Engineering constraints limit parallel development of iOS, Android, and Web. | **Build a mobile-responsive web app first.** | Responsive PWA (Progressive Web App) | Deliver core value to parents on mobile without native app overhead. | Mobile vs Desktop usage ratio. |
