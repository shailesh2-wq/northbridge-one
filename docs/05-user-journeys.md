# User Journeys — NorthBridge One

> [!NOTE]
> Based on secondary research and simulated stakeholder discovery. No real interviews were conducted. This is a portfolio prototype.

## 1. Teacher identifies and acts on student concern

**Scenario:** Priya (Grade 8 Teacher) notices Alex's attendance and engagement dropping.
*   **Steps:**
    1. Priya opens NorthBridge One to check her daily schedule.
    2. Her dashboard shows an 'Attention Signal' for Alex: "Attendance down 15% + Missed 2 assignments".
    3. Priya clicks the signal, opening Alex's 'Student 360' profile.
    4. She reviews the unified timeline and sees Alex also received a behavior warning in P.E. yesterday.
    5. Understanding this is a broader issue, she clicks 'Create Intervention'.
    6. She logs her concern and assigns a follow-up task to the School Counsellor.
    7. She drafts a quick, template-assisted message to Alex's parents asking for a brief check-in.
    8. Later that week, she checks the 'Action Center' and sees the Counsellor has met with Alex and updated the intervention status.
*   **Screens Visited:** Teacher Dashboard, Student 360, Intervention Modal, Messaging, Action Center.
*   **Actions Taken:** Review signal, analyze context, log intervention, assign task, message parent, track status.
*   **Information Accessed:** Attendance trends, academic records, behavioral history, communication history.
*   **Emotional State:** Shifts from concerned and overwhelmed to empowered and relieved that the issue is being handled collaboratively.
*   **Pain Points in Current State:** Priya would have had to manually check the LMS, email the P.E. teacher, email the counsellor, and hope someone followed up.
*   **How NorthBridge One Improves It:** Surfaces the hidden pattern proactively, provides full context instantly, and creates an accountable workflow for resolution.

## 2. Principal monitors school health

**Scenario:** Dr. Chen (Principal) checks the morning operational status.
*   **Steps:**
    1. Dr. Chen opens the NorthBridge One Leadership Dashboard on his tablet over coffee.
    2. He sees the top-level metrics: Overall Attendance, Active Interventions, Staff Absences.
    3. He notices a red indicator: "High volume of unexcused absences in Grade 8".
    4. He clicks to drill down and views the Grade 8 summary.
    5. He sees a list of affected students and notes that interventions have already been logged for most of them by their homeroom teachers.
    6. He identifies a gap: two students have no interventions logged.
    7. He clicks a quick action to nudge the respective Vice-Principal to review those two students.
    8. He closes the app, confident in the day's operations.
*   **Screens Visited:** Leadership Dashboard, Cohort View, Quick Action Modal.
*   **Actions Taken:** Review high-level metrics, drill into anomalies, send nudge.
*   **Information Accessed:** Aggregated attendance data, intervention statuses.
*   **Emotional State:** Calm, informed, in control.
*   **Pain Points in Current State:** He wouldn't know about the Grade 8 attendance spike until a weekly report was generated days later.
*   **How NorthBridge One Improves It:** Provides real-time, actionable insights and allows him to verify that his team is handling issues without micromanaging.

## 3. Parent checks child's status

**Scenario:** David (Parent) wants a quick update on Alex's day.
*   **Steps:**
    1. David receives a push notification: "New message from Ms. Sharma".
    2. He opens the NorthBridge One Parent App.
    3. The home screen shows Alex's status: Present today, Next class: Science.
    4. He navigates to the Messages tab and reads Priya's check-in request.
    5. He replies directly in the app, confirming a time to chat.
    6. Before closing, he checks the 'Recent Activity' feed.
    7. He sees Alex scored an 85% on a recent Math quiz.
    8. He feels informed and prepared to talk to Alex after school.
*   **Screens Visited:** Parent Dashboard, Messages, Student Activity Feed.
*   **Actions Taken:** Read message, reply to message, review recent academic updates.
*   **Information Accessed:** Daily attendance, direct communications, recent grades.
*   **Emotional State:** Appreciative, connected, informed.
*   **Pain Points in Current State:** David would have to log into an email client for the message and a separate clunky portal to check the Math grade.
*   **How NorthBridge One Improves It:** Consolidates all parent-school interactions into a single, user-friendly feed.

## 4. Administrator resolves operational issue

**Scenario:** Sarah (Admin) needs to handle a missing attendance register.
*   **Steps:**
    1. Sarah monitors the Admin Dashboard at 9:15 AM.
    2. A system signal appears: "Missing attendance: Period 1, Mr. Davis".
    3. Instead of drafting an email, she clicks 'Investigate' next to the signal.
    4. The system shows Mr. Davis is marked present today, so he just forgot.
    5. She clicks 'Send Reminder'. The system sends an automated push notification to Mr. Davis's app.
    6. Five minutes later, the signal clears automatically as Mr. Davis submits the register.
    7. She moves on to the next item in her Action Center.
*   **Screens Visited:** Admin Dashboard, Action Center.
*   **Actions Taken:** Review operational signal, verify context, trigger automated reminder, monitor resolution.
*   **Information Accessed:** Staff attendance, register submission status.
*   **Emotional State:** Efficient, unstressed.
*   **Pain Points in Current State:** Sarah would have to manually cross-reference the staff absence list, open her email, type out a reminder, and manually check back later.
*   **How NorthBridge One Improves It:** Automates the detection and resolution of routine operational bottlenecks.
